The Challenge

Man Overboard: A Deadly Reality

Every year, hundreds of maritime workers fall overboard. Time is the critical factor between rescue and tragedy. Traditional detection methods are unreliable and slow.

The Problem

Current man overboard detection relies on visual observation and manual headcounts — methods that fail especially at night, in rough seas, or on large vessels.

  • Average detection time: 15+ minutes
  • 25% of MOB incidents go unnoticed
  • Survival window: under 1 hour in cold water
  • No real-time crew position tracking

Our Solution

MOB Guard uses wearable IoT sensors with GPS, accelerometer, and water-contact detection to automatically identify MOB events and trigger instant emergency protocols.

  • Automatic detection in under 3 seconds
  • GPS position marked at time of fall
  • Real-time crew health monitoring
  • IAMSAR-compliant rescue protocols
Capabilities

Built for Maritime Safety

A comprehensive platform designed to protect crew members with cutting-edge sensor technology and intelligent alert systems.

Real-Time GPS Tracking

Track every crew member's position in real-time with sub-meter accuracy. Automatic geofence monitoring and boundary alerts.

Instant MOB Detection

Multi-sensor fusion detects man overboard events in under 3 seconds. Water-contact sensors, accelerometers, and GPS all work together.

Biometric Monitoring

Monitor crew vitals including heart rate, body temperature, and fatigue indicators. Early warning for health emergencies.

LoRa Mesh Network

Long-range, low-power radio mesh ensures connectivity across the entire vessel. Works even when cellular and Wi-Fi are unavailable.

Multi-Channel Alerts

Simultaneous alerts via bridge alarm, SMS, email, push notification, and onboard PA system. No alert goes unnoticed.

SAR Pattern Planning

Automated search and rescue pattern calculation based on drift, current, and wind data. Williamson, Anderson, and Scharnow turns.
